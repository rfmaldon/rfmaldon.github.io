Alyra

Return to Rhythm

Packaging Design · Brand Identity · App UI

A circadian-wellness brand built on one idea: your body already runs on a schedule, most supplement brands just ignore it. The product line — Night Reset, Daily Reset, Morning Balance — is organized by time of day rather than ingredient, and the identity was explored in two parallel material directions: a cooler, app-driven navy system, and a warmer, spa-adjacent line in dusty blue with a gold-foil tideline.
