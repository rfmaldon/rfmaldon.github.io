Package Design

Identity & Packaging Systems

Three full-system packaging identities, each carried end-to-end from brand mark through retail box, campaign photography, and — where the product calls for it — the app that lives alongside it. Design decisions get made once, at the identity level, then get pushed all the way through to the shelf.

01

Aeroform 01

Move Aware — Open-Ear Audio

Bone-conduction running headphones built around one glowing ember-orange arc, carried from product to packaging to campaign.

02

Alyra

Return to Rhythm

A circadian-wellness brand organized by time of day, not ingredient — packaging, app, and campaign in one system.

03

Noctra P1

Portable Cinema

A pocket projector positioned as precision hardware — anodized aluminum, laser-etched detail, and a flagship-launch packaging system.
